02 / 06 — Our story

Design and build, under one roof. We build relationships first — so we can deliver a truly innovative design-and-build solution for your workplace.

We are equipped with the latest multi-functional machines and equipment — high-performance hydraulic bending machines, high-quality PVC edge banding machines and other modern industrial equipment — and we specialise in steel fabrication with new designs.

We practise workplace consultancy. By analysing the impact of space on your people, technology and business processes, we develop a full understanding of the objectives behind your relocation or refurbishment.

We are committed to long-term partnerships with our clients, creating robust, flexible solutions that address the changing needs of your business’s future.

04 / 06 — Leadership

Mr. Ravi
Geeganage

Chairman

Mr. Geeganage comes from a multi-disciplinary background in the office interior and stainless steel industries. He has handled a wide variety of office interior and stainless steel works and has served the international market for interior furnishing requirements.

A passion for design and interior spaces led him to design the interior and stainless steel works at Cinnamon hotels in the Maldives in 2017. With a natural taste for good design, his team has transformed numerous interior spaces.

He enrolled in the National Diploma programme at the University of Moratuwa before leaving to pursue his business ventures.

About us

A complete partner for your space.

Fabrication, aluminium, glass and interior expertise in one company — designed, made and installed by one team.

Our story

At Lotek we understand how important it is to build relationships and maintain them with you and your business, so we can deliver a truly innovative design-and-build solution for your workplace.

We practise workplace consultancy, because the heart of every organisation is its culture, its workplace and its people. By analysing the impact of space on your people, technology and business processes, we understand the objectives behind your relocation or refurbishment.

We are committed to long-term partnerships, creating robust, flexible solutions that address the changing needs of your business.

Vision

To be a leading and trusted provider of custom-designed space management solutions, by providing world-class manufacturing of premium-quality products with excellent customer service.

Mission

Blending contemporary business solutions with innovative designs, to meet customer satisfaction.

Leadership

Mr. Ravi Geeganage

Chairman

Mr. Geeganage comes from a multi-disciplinary background in the office interior and stainless steel industries, and has served the international market for interior furnishing requirements.

A passion for design led him to design the interior and stainless steel works at Cinnamon hotels in the Maldives in 2017. With a natural taste for good design, his team has transformed numerous interior spaces.

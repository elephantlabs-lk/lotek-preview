02 / 06 — Introduction

Aluminium is light, strong and resistant to corrosion, which makes it ideal for doors, windows and partitions in Sri Lanka's climate.

We fabricate and install aluminium systems for offices, retail and homes, from framed glass partitions and cubicles to doors, windows and cupboards.

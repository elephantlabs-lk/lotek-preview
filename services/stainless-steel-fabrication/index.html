02 / 06 — Introduction

Steel fabrication is where Lotek began, and it remains at the core of what we do. From balustrades that follow a spiral staircase to stainless steel kitchen lines for busy commercial kitchens, every piece is measured, fabricated and finished for its space.

Our workshop is equipped with hydraulic bending machines and modern fabrication equipment, so we can design and build custom pieces rather than adapt standard ones.
